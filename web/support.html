Signing in

My code didn't arrive

  • Wait a minute, then check Junk and Quarantine in Outlook. UW mail sometimes holds new senders there.
  • Make sure you typed your full @uwaterloo.ca address.
  • Tap "Send a new code". Only the newest code works, and each one expires after 10 minutes.

It says BigBack is only for UW students

Sign up needs a @uwaterloo.ca email address. More campuses are coming.

Your account

Delete my account

In the app: You → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, posts, photos and rankings.

Get a copy of my data

Email us from your UW address and we'll send it within 30 days.

Make my profile private

You → Settings → Private profile. New followers then need your approval.
