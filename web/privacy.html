Privacy Policy

Effective [date] · Version 2026-10

BigBack is operated by [Operator name] ("we", "us") in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and what you control. Questions: [support email]. Our privacy officer is [Privacy officer name].

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your UW email, your profile, what you post and who you follow.
  • Your posts are seen by the people you choose: your followers, or anyone signed in if your profile is public.
  • We use your location to find places near you. We don't keep a history of where you've been, and nobody else sees your live location.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, at any time.

Who can see your content

  • Public profile: any signed-in BigBack user can see your posts and rankings.
  • Private profile: only followers you approve can see them.
  • Tagged meals and plans: the people in that meal or plan see it. Anyone with an invite link can see that plan's details.
  • Campus scores: your ratings count toward a place's anonymous UW score. Your name isn't shown with it.
  • People you block can't see your profile or content, and you won't see theirs.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos and rankings are deleted immediately. Copies in backups are gone within [30] days.
  • Server logs are kept for up to [90] days.
  • Reports about other users may be kept longer when we need them to keep the community safe.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: You → Account → Delete account.
  • Access, correct or download your data: edit your profile in the app, or email us for a copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off location, contacts or notifications in iOS Settings at any time. The rest of the app keeps working.
  • Control who sees you: make your profile private, block people, and report anything that breaks our Community Guidelines.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Quebec or California), you may have more rights. Email us and we'll help.
  • If you're unhappy with how we handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Age

BigBack is for university students aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is limited by strict per-user rules in our database, and sign-in uses one-time email codes, not passwords. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that puts you at real risk, we'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
